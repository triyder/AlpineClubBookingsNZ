- **Maintenance-window deploys check that the old runtime and database clients have stopped (#3963, #4071).** Before applying a pending windowed migration, the production deploy now refuses while any app/web replica is running or another client remains connected to the app database, including idle and external clients. Failed or unknown discovery and drain checks also refuse. Keep the offline window until the new runtime is ready: these checks are snapshots and do not disconnect clients or prevent them reconnecting.
