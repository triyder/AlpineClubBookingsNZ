- **Officers can add an existing booking to a work party** (fork #119). For a
  member who forgot to tick "I am attending a working bee", a Booking Officer
  or Full Admin opens the booking and clicks **Add to work party** in its admin
  tools. They choose the work party (only ones that overlap the stay and are at
  the booking's lodge can be chosen) and see the new total before confirming.
  The booking is repriced exactly as if the box had been ticked: an unpaid
  booking simply owes less, and on a paid booking everything paid above the new
  total goes back as account credit, in full, with no change fee and never as a
  refund. The change goes through the normal booking change, so the history,
  ledger and Xero record it like any other.
