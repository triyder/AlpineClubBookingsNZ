- **Adding or editing an other lodge now happens in a popup (#51).** On **Admin → Lodges → Other lodges**, **Add** and **Edit** open the form in a dialog over the list instead of expanding a panel above it. **Cancel**, **Escape** and clicking outside close it without saving, the close button is hidden while a save is in progress, an error shows inside the dialog above **Save**, and focus returns to the button you opened it from. The list refreshes as soon as a save succeeds, and the most recent refresh always wins. Nothing about what can be saved has changed.
