- **Emails that tell you what to pay now quote what you actually have to pay (#4058).** The "your request was approved" email for a booking held for a policy exception, and the two payment-link emails (booking request approved, and pay for your guests), quoted the booking's full price, even when the member had account credit applied or the booking carried a recorded change fee. They now give the same amount the payment page asks for (price plus any recorded change fee, less applied credit), so the email and the page agree. The "Price" row of the booking-request-approved email is now "Amount due", and club-edited wording gains an `{{amountDue}}` token (`{{price}}` still means the booking price). The exception-approved email shows no payment line when credit covers it all.
