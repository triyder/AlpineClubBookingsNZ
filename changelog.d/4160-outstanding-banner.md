- **A booking's confirmation shows additional money and how to pay (#4047).**
  This includes an Internet Banking correction on a finished stay. Additional card
  asks point the member to their pay card. Supplementary bank invoices show
  their remaining amounts, invoice references and when they were checked, with
  **Check more invoices** for larger groups. Unissued or unavailable amounts
  say they cannot yet be confirmed; the page no longer reassures a member that
  no payment was required while an addition is outstanding or uncertain.
  Officers see a read-only view and linked guests a neutral confirmation.
