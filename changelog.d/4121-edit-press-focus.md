- **Pressing Edit in an admin settings section now moves keyboard focus into
  the section (#4115).** In most settings sections, pressing Edit used to drop
  keyboard and screen-reader users to the top of the page, because the Edit
  button they had just pressed disappeared. Focus now lands in the section:
  on the first field that Edit made editable, or on the section itself when
  there is no such field. Add and a row's Edit for booking periods and
  minimum-stay policies now put focus in the form they open. Allocation
  preferences and club identity keep their current behaviour. Nothing needs
  configuring.
