- **Correcting a school request now counts its unnamed adults when it says
  whether the lodge has room.** The availability shown after an officer's
  correction left out the adults still to be named; it now counts them, as
  holding the beds already did.
- **A school booking awaiting its invoice now needs a hut leader.** Once the
  school accepts and its booking is confirmed, its nights show on the
  dashboard while nobody covers them, and a club member on the school booking
  can be assigned to them, by hand or by the nightly job. A quote the school
  has not accepted yet does neither. The nightly job no
  longer stretches a member's assignment into school nights the lodge does not
  accept them for. A season-long custodian no longer blocks the leader you add
  for school nights when the lodge leaves the custodian unticked, as long as
  that leader is staying those nights and the lodge accepts them; a second
  custodian is still blocked. Adding or
  changing a leader whose nights include school nights the lodge does not
  accept them for saves with a note saying so. Teachers become hut leaders at approval only
  while the Hut leaders module is on, and a teacher's assignment can no longer
  be marked as custodian or hold a bed.
- **A lodge custodian who has also booked a stay is listed once on the member
  roster and the lobby screen (#3820).** On their custodian nights they are shown
  as the custodian, and their booking still lists the rest of their party. On
  nights the whole lodge is privately booked, the custodian is still named beside
  the reserved line. The dashboard card now reads **Nights without a hut leader
  who counts**.
