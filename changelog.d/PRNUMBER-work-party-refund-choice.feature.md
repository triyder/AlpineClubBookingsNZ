- **Add to work party: refund to card, organiser-paid joiners, and not on your
  own bookings** (fork #129). On a booking paid by card, the officer now
  chooses whether the discount comes back as club credit (still the default)
  or as a refund to the card; either way it comes back in full, with no change
  fee. Bookings paid by Internet Banking or with account credit still get club
  credit only. A joiner's booking that the group organiser paid for can now be
  added: the money goes back to the organiser, as with any change to such a
  booking. An officer can no longer use it on their own booking or a booking
  of someone in their family group. The refund is the normal booking change's
  own refund, recorded in the history, ledger and Xero like any other.
