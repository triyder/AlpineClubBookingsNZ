- **The admin sidebar now opens on a short menu for the work each role does (#108).** Treasurers and Finance Viewers get a **Finance Panel** (Finance Dashboard, the Finance pages, Subscriptions, Integrations and the money queues); Booking Officers get a **Bookings Panel** (Bookings & Beds, Bookings Setup, Rates & Policies, Finance, Lodge Operations and the booking queues). Full Admins and view-only admins keep the full menu unchanged. The menu is chosen from each role's permission areas, so club-made roles follow the same rule; anyone whose areas span menus gets a switcher, and every menu has a **Full admin** button. The menus are display only — page access is unchanged. The `/finance` workspace now shows the same sidebar, and staff pages (`/admin` and `/finance`) leave Book, My Bookings and Who's at the lodge out of the top bar.
